Process

From first visit
to final cushion

Four stages, one lead designer and a fixed fee agreed before any work starts.

Week 1 1
  1. Week 1 · On site

    Discovery visit

    We measure the space, photograph the light at different hours and walk through how you use every room, what stays and what goes.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Concept & materials

    A moodboard, two layout options and a sample box of timbers, fabrics and finishes you can hold in the actual room.

  3. Weeks 3–5

    Drawings & sourcing

    Joinery drawings, a lighting plan and a costed schedule of every item, so you approve the full budget before anything is ordered.

  4. Weeks 6–10

    Build & styling

    We run the trades on site with weekly progress reports, then spend a full day installing and styling before we hand you the keys.

Questions

Before you
book

Anything else, put it in your brief below and we'll answer it on the call.

Do you work outside Lagos?+

Yes, across Nigeria. Outside Lagos we plan extra site visits into the fee and run weekly video walkthroughs between them.

Can you design just one room?+

Yes. A single living room, bedroom or kitchen is our most common project and usually takes six to ten weeks.

How is the design fee charged?+

A fixed fee agreed after the discovery visit, paid in three stages. Furniture and materials are billed at trade price with every receipt shared.

Do you build outdoor spaces and cabins too?+

Yes. We design decks, pools, garden studios and glamping units, and manage the builders through to the final fit-out.

Can you furnish a short-let ready for guests?+

Yes. We spec durable fabrics, stock the full guest inventory and style for listing photos, so it can take bookings from handover.
